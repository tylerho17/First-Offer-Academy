# First Offer Academy — Brand Guide (updated Sept 21, 2026)

Direction: warm like a mentor, serious like an offer letter. Kind to students, credible to parents paying $5,000.

## Palette
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Mist blue | #E7EDF5 | Page/flyer background (~50%) |
| Primary | Navy | #1A2B48 | Headlines, dark bands, footer, Apply button (~20%) |
| Text | Black | #000000 | Body copy, fine print, captions, form labels on light ground (~10%) |
| Surface | Cream | #F7F4EE | Cards, FAQ panels, quote blocks, print back side, and text on navy (instead of pure white) (~12%) |
| Accent | Sage | #A8C4A4 | Price box, offer seal, highlights, buttons on navy bands. One accent per page (≤8%) |
| Support | Dusty blue | #3F6690 | Links, small labels, icons, focus rings |

### Contrast (WCAG)
- Navy on mist: ~12:1. Navy on cream: ~13:1. Cream on navy: ~13:1. All pass AAA.
- Black on mist: ~18:1. Black on cream: ~19:1. Black on sage: ~11:1. All pass AAA.
- Navy on sage: ~7.5:1. Passes AAA; sage fills take navy (or black) text.
- Black on navy: ~1.5:1. Fails. Nothing black ever sits on a navy band.
- Sage on mist: ~1.6:1. Fails. Sage never carries text or stands alone as a button on the light ground; on mist it is a filled shape with navy text/border only.

### Pairing rules
- Navy = headlines and structure; black = reading text. Never set a headline in black or a paragraph in navy within the same block.
- Black is text only: no black fills, bands, buttons, or borders. Navy owns every dark shape.
- Cream sits on mist as a raised surface (cards, panels), never as an alternating full-width section band next to mist.
- Navy bands: cream text, sage for the single CTA or price highlight.
- No pure white anywhere; cream is the white.

## Type
- Display: Young Serif (headlines, prices, big numbers), set in navy. One weight.
- Body/UI: Hanken Grotesk, set in black on light ground, cream on navy.
- Web sizes: H1 56px desktop / 38px mobile, H2 36px, body 18px, buttons 16px/600.

## Buttons
- Primary (on light ground): solid navy pill, cream text. Once per screen.
- Primary on navy bands: sage fill, navy text.
- Secondary: transparent, 1.5px navy border, navy text.
- Text link: #3F6690, underlined.

## Photo
Real students in everyday clothes, daylight, campus. AI images only as labeled placeholders; never presented as actual students. Founder headshot: background removed, placed on cream or mist.

## Voice
Specific numbers stated calmly (24 seats, 8 students coached). No "guaranteed," no "elite," no firm logos without student permission, no ITG name/logo, written permission for every testimonial.

## Format
- 12 weeks. Weekly 90-minute group session + a **weekly 60-minute 1:1** (12 total) + a pod of three.
- Tracks: Finance, Consulting, Marketing, Tech. Shared through Week 8, split for Weeks 9–10, rejoin for Weeks 11–12.
- Finals week and holiday weeks run async: no session, minimums still due.
- Accountability rule: two missed weekly minimums in a row triggers a student + parent call.
- Parents get a one-page progress report every two weeks, plus the Week 12 family results meeting.

### Standard session format (90 min)
| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Scoreboard | 0–15 | Each student reads their numbers aloud: sent, replies, calls, level. No excuses, just numbers. |
| Teach | 15–40 | One skill, taught from the week's module. |
| Live reps | 40–80 | Drills in pairs or hot seat: emails rewritten live, mock calls, mock interviews. |
| Commit | 80–90 | Each student states their exact deliverable and deadline for the week. |

### Weekly 1:1 format (60 min)
| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Tracker review | 0–15 | Line by line: sends, replies, follow-ups due, calls booked. |
| Biggest bottleneck | 15–40 | Fix the one thing slowing them down most (reply rate, call quality, stories, technicals). |
| Work on the work | 40–55 | Edit live: resume bullets, emails, stories, "why" answers, or a mini-mock. |
| Next 7 days | 55–60 | Exact commitments written into the tracker. |

## Week by week (Tyler's v1 curriculum, now updated)

### Week 1 — The Game
- **Objective:** kill the idea that recruiting rewards intelligence. It rewards persistence.
- **Teach:** the 5 things that matter, ranked: networking, resume, enthusiasm, behaviorals, technicals **(Tyler)**. Why clubs matter less than people think. How the freshman/sophomore market actually works: boutiques, middle market, local firms, startups, early-insight and diversity programs.
- **Live reps:** each student answers "tell me about yourself" cold, recorded. This is the Week 12 baseline.
- **Deliverable:** resume v1 · answers to 25 self-questions (raw material for stories) · baseline assessment.
- **Coach questions:** What would have to be true in 12 weeks for you to call this a win? How many hours a week can you honestly commit? What have you been avoiding?

### Week 2 — Resume & Story
- **Objective:** a top-tier resume that passes the rubric and a 60-second intro that doesn't sound rehearsed.
- **Teach:** the resume rubric line by line. Bullets as outcomes, not duties. The intro structure: where you're from, what pulled you in, what you've done, why you're talking to them.
- **Live reps:** peers swap and grade resumes against the rubric; intros are delivered and cut down in hot seat.
- **Deliverable:** resume passes the rubric · 60-second intro recorded · target list of 50 companies with contacts → **Level 1**.

### Week 3 — Target List & the AI Outreach System
- **Objective:** a working outreach machine and the first 50 sequenced emails out the door.
- **Teach:** the tiered target list: A (dream firms, for relationships), B (realistic: boutiques, middle market, local firms, startups), C (safe) **(Tyler)**. Sourcing contacts with tools like Apollo or RecruitEm. Building the AI email automation: personalization prompts, mail-merge/sequencing, and follow-up steps. The tracker: only track people who reply **(Tyler)**.
- **Live reps:** every student builds their sequence live and sends before leaving.
- **Deliverable:** automation live · 50 sequenced emails sent → **Level 2**.

### Week 4 — Cold Email That Gets Answered
- **Objective:** move the reply rate toward 10%.
- **Teach:** the Two C's: compliment and connection. People help people who remind them of themselves **(Tyler)**. Subject lines, length, the one ask. Follow-up cadence: at most 4 follow-ups; weekly in Nov/Dec, biweekly to monthly off-peak **(Tyler)**. Guardrails on AI: AI drafts, humans personalize; never send an email you haven't read.
- **Live reps:** the worst email of the week is rewritten live on screen; the group votes on subject lines.
- **Deliverable:** 100 total sent · follow-up cadence loaded · externship 1 started.

### Week 5 — The Call
- **Objective:** turn replies into calls people remember.
- **Teach:** the call framework **(Tyler)**. Open with "anything exciting lately," answer long and casually yourself to set the tone, then move to your intro and their story. 80% listening, 20% talking. Questions that prove you were listening. Prep with AI on the firm and the person. Send the thank-you 1–2 hours after. The biggest failure mode is being dry.
- **Live reps:** mock calls in pairs, then hot seat with the coach playing a distracted associate.
- **Deliverable:** 150 sent · 3 calls · thank-yous sent → **Level 3**.

### Week 6 — Converting Calls Into Referrals
- **Objective:** calls that lead to the next door.
- **Teach:** the referral close. Never ask for a referral directly. Ask "Is there anyone on the team you'd be comfortable introducing me to?" or "Is there another perspective you think would be good for me to hear?" **(Tyler)**. Staying warm for months. Logging every relationship.
- **Live reps:** practice the close until it sounds natural; role-play the awkward "no."
- **Deliverable:** 200 sent · 5 calls · the close used on every call · first intro earned.

### Week 7 — Behavioral Stories
- **Objective:** 8 stories chosen for uniqueness, not impressiveness.
- **Teach:** mining stories from the Week 1 self-questions. Structure: situation, what you did, what changed, what you learned. One story, many questions: map each story to leadership, failure, conflict, teamwork, initiative, and why-this-field.
- **Live reps:** each student tells two stories; peers guess which question each one answers.
- **Deliverable:** 250 sent · 8 stories written, recorded, and mapped · externship 1 completed.

### Week 8 — Why This Industry, Why This Firm
- **Objective:** enthusiasm that reads as real.
- **Teach:** the 3-bucket "why" **(Tyler)**: (1) mentality, (2) 2–3 specific things about the work, (3) the people and their standards. Firm-specific answers built from calls already had. The sector and company questions that catch students off guard.
- **Live reps:** rapid-fire "why us" drills across firms on their own target lists.
- **Deliverable:** 300 sent · "why" pitch recorded · 2 referrals/intros → **Level 4** · externship 2 started.

### Week 9 — Technicals (track split)
- **Finance:** study order: accounting → valuation → enterprise vs. equity value → M&A → LBO **(Tyler)**. Say answers out loud, work on paper, use AI to close gaps.
- **Consulting:** case structure, frameworks, market sizing, mental math.
- **Marketing:** portfolio pieces, metrics that matter, brand teardown.
- **Tech:** one strong project, coding/SQL/product question practice, referral strategy.
- **Live reps:** timed drills; students grade each other.
- **Deliverable:** 350 sent · technical baseline quiz passed at the track threshold.

### Week 10 — Mock Interviews I (track split)
- **Objective:** a full interview under pressure, recorded and graded.
- **Teach:** interview flow, pacing, recovering from a blank, questions to ask at the end.
- **Live reps:** a full 30-minute mock per student (behavioral, why, technical/case), graded on a written scorecard.
- **Deliverable:** 400 sent · 1 graded mock passed · applications submitted to every open role on the target list.

### Week 11 — Interview Execution
- **Objective:** real interviews, handled like a pro.
- **Teach:** running multiple processes at once. Final-round and superday prep. Declining without burning a relationship (frame it as fit, not preference). Pre-interview research on the firm and the interviewer.
- **Live reps:** a second full mock with a harder interviewer (a stranger), in a different format.
- **Deliverable:** 500+ sent · 2 graded mocks passed · externship 2 completed · first rounds completed where available → **Level 5**.

### Week 12 — Results & the 6-Month Game Plan
- **Objective:** close the program and keep the engine running.
- **Teach:** evaluating and accepting offers. Keeping networks warm after the search. What sophomore/junior recruiting looks like and when it starts.
- **Live reps:** each student re-records "tell me about yourself" and watches it next to the Week 1 baseline.
- **Family results meeting (30 min):** each student presents their numbers (emails sent, calls, referrals, interviews, externships, level, outcome). Parents see the before-and-after.
- **Deliverable:** final results report · **6-month game plan** (monthly targets, contacts to keep warm, applications calendar, skills to build).
